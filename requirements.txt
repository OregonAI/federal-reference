# Runtime + tooling dependencies for this corpus.
#
# The [mcp] extra installs the MCP SDK that `corpus-mcp-serve` imports. Without it the CLI
# is on PATH but fails at import, so it must be present for the container to serve.
#
# v1.12.0 is a hard floor, and both reasons are specific to this corpus:
#
#   * v1.12.0  the `federal_instrument` doc_type. Nothing here can validate without it.
#              `external_reference` cannot substitute: the schema forces
#              content_mode: summary on it, and most instruments here are 17 U.S.C. § 105
#              works we may reproduce in full. That type split is also how the copyright
#              determination gets ENFORCED rather than merely recorded — federal_instrument
#              means "we may reproduce this", external_reference means "we may not".
#   * v1.11.0  mcp.extra_document_fields. This corpus leans on it harder than any other:
#              `as_of` and `amended_on` are what let a caller tell current text from the
#              text in force when a citing rule was written. Invisible, that comparison is
#              impossible rather than merely inconvenient — and getting it wrong here means
#              answering a compliance question with law that postdates the question.
corpus-toolkit[mcp,semantic] @ git+https://github.com/OregonAI/corpus-toolkit@v1.28.0
pyyaml
